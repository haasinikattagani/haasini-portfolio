Haasini – About me

IIIT Hyderabad · B.Tech CS and MS in Computational Natural Science · 2025-30

I am currently a student at the International Institute of Information Technology, Hyderabad (IIIT-H), pursuing Computer Science and Natural Sciences (CND). I am actively exploring technology and continuously working on improving my programming skills. Over the past two semesters, I have worked towards building a strong technical foundation through coursework in C, Python, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. My coursework includes implementing data structures and algorithms, and working on projects in software systems and scientific computing, such as molecular simulations using VMD, Gaussian, and other software tools.


I enjoy learning new concepts and understanding their real-world applications. While I may slow down at times, I make a conscious effort to stay consistent and keep improving step by step. I view challenges and failures as an essential part of growth and strive to overcome them with persistence. In my academic journey, I have consistently performed well, securing 996/1000 in the Intermediate Public Examinations (2025) and emerging as a state topper. I also performed well in JEE and secured admission into IIIT Hyderabad through the Board Toppers Mode after successfully clearing the institute’s interview process.


Alongside my academics, I am a trained Kuchipudi dancer with over 10 years of experience and 300+ performances across India and internationally, including at the Indian Consulate in Dubai. I have represented Telangana at the National Kala Utsav (state-level winner) and received multiple national-level recognitions, including at Balotsav. I have performed at prestigious venues such as Raj Bhavan and Rashtrapati Nilayam, and was part of the 100-Day Bharat Dance Festival world record event. I have completed a Diploma in Kuchipudi and a Certificate Course with Distinction from Potti Sri Ramulu Telugu University. I am also a recipient of the CCRT Scholarship from the Ministry of Culture, Government of India, and have completed a five-level Sanskrit course (Subhashitham Samskrutham) from IIT Roorkee.
